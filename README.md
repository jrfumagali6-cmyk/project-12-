Hello everyone,

Today, I am bringing a new project about fuels!!!!!! A short explanation: fuels are a sensitive topic in my country, Brazil. The reason for that is that the matrix transportation is made up of 70% highways. According to CNT (National Transport Board), there are about 1.2 million truck drivers in Brazil. So, even slight changes in fuel prices can cause serious problems in Brazil. Therefore, I decided to do a project about Value at Risk considering fuel prices in Brazil. Let's imagine that a small gas station owner decides to buy X thousand reais of gasoline, diesel, ethanol (or any type of fuel). Will he take a loss? So, I decided to implement a VaR using historical, normal (Gaussian), and t-student approaches. I know there are other possible distributions. 

Moreover, I don't have time to fix the selectbox that allows you to choose the Brazilian states and fuels. So, it is necessary to type the state and fuel in the box: please use these names, according to the ANP database = ACRE, ALAGOAS, AMAPA, AMAZONAS, BAHIA, CEARA, ESPIRITO SANTO, GOIAS, MARANHAO, MATO GROSSO, MATO GROSSO DO SUL, MINAS GERAIS, PARAIBA, PARANA, PERNAMBUCO, PIAUI, RIO DE JANEIRO, RIO GRANDE DO NORTE, RIO GRANDE DO SUL, RONDONIA, RORAIMA, SANTA CATARINA, SAO PAULO, SERGIPE, TOCANTINS e DISTRITO FEDERAL. Types of fuel: GASOLINA COMUM, ETANOL HIDRATADO, OLEO DIESEL, OLEO DIESEL S10, GNV, GLP AND GASOLINA ADITIVADA. Two fuels (OLEO DIESEL S10 and GASOLINA ADITIVADA) JUST HAVE INFORMATION FROM DEZ/2012 and JAN/2013, respectively. The first because DIESEL S10 was implemented in Brazil at the end of 2012, and the second, GASOLINA ADITIVADA, just appeared on the ANP database in JAN/2013.

This is the first part of my project about fuels; the second part I will include CVaR, stress test, and backtest.
