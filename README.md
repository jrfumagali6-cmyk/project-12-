Today, I am bringing a new project about fuels!!!!!! A short explanation: fuels are a sensitive topic in my country, Brazil. The reason for that is that the matrix transportation is made up of 70% highways. According to CNT (National Transport Board), there are about 1.2 million truck drivers in Brazil. So, even slight changes in fuel prices can cause serious problems in Brazil. Therefore, I decided to do a project about Value at Risk considering fuel prices in Brazil. Let's imagine that a small gas station owner decides to buy X thousand reais of gasoline, diesel, ethanol (or any type of fuel). Will he take a loss? So, I decided to implement a VaR using historical, normal (Gaussian), and t-student approaches. I know there are other possible distributions.
